About the institute stage of the "Book is my friend" contest

05.11.2024 11:47:47 202

The "Book is my friend" Republic held among students at the Namangan State Pedagogical Institute
meet the winners of the institute stage of the competition!




The competition was held in 2 stages based on 3 conditions.

Students who scored high in the condition "The most knowledgeable reader" competed in the conditions of "Annotation skill" and "Wide promotion of reading and reading culture".

In the end, the following winners were determined:

1st place, Faculty of Pedagogy, primary education, 3rd grade student Khabibullayeva Madina Gaybullo girl

2nd place, Faculty of Social Sciences, National idea, spiritual foundations and legal education, 3rd stage student Mahkamov Mahmudjon Ghalibjon's son

3rd place, Faculty of Pedagogy, Preschool education, 1st stage student Yoldosheva Umida Khusnidin's daughter

At the regional level, the honor of the institute will be defended by Khabibullayeva Madina Gaybullo's daughter. We wish Madina good luck and success in the next stage!

We thank all our students for their participation and wish them success in their future activities!
