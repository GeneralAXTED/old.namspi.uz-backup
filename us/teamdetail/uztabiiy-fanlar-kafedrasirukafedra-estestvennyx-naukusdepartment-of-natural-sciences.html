Job description of the Head of Department
Approval of the department's work plan, individual work plans of the faculty and staff, as well as other documents within the scope of the department's activities;
Distribution of the teaching workload among the department's academic staff and monitoring its timely and high-quality implementation;
Ensuring the competitive selection of academic staff for the department, and submitting proposals to the HEI leadership in the prescribed manner regarding material and moral incentives, as well as the application of disciplinary actions;
Preparation of the department's annual activity report within the prescribed procedures and deadlines, and reporting at the meetings of the Academic Council;
Conducting research activities in accordance with the approved plan, and ensuring the integration of completed research projects with education, science, and industry;
Reviewing the educational, scientific-methodological, research, spiritual-educational, and organizational-methodological activities in the individual work plans of the department's faculty; organizing efforts to study, synthesize, and disseminate the best practices of experienced educators;
Implementing modern pedagogical and information-communication technologies, assisting junior faculty in acquiring teaching skills and expertise, and developing an information database; establishing the effective use of modern educational technical tools, individual learning methods, and independent study techniques in the classroom; submitting proposals for organizing classes in specialized lecture halls equipped with modern computer hardware; and facilitating the development of distance learning elements;
Training of scientific and pedagogical staff, discussing candidate and doctoral dissertations in the department's specializations or related fields at scientific seminars, and participating in expert groups to determine the compliance of educational documents with regulatory requirements;
Placement of graduates into employment, formulating topics for undergraduate qualification works and master's dissertations, attracting extra-budgetary funds, and establishing partnerships with industrial enterprises and other organizations to provide scientific and technical assistance;
Organizing activities aimed at the professional development of specialists in the department's field of specialization, and developing the educational service delivery system.

DUTIES OF THE HEAD OF THE DEPARTMENT:

Approval of the department work plan, personal work plans of professors and staff, and other documents within the scope of the department's activities;
Distribution of educational workloads to the teaching staff of the department and control over their timely and high-quality implementation;
Ensuring the selection of teaching staff to the department, their material and moral reward, as well as submitting proposals to the management of the university in accordance with the established procedure on the application of disciplinary measures;
Preparing a report on the activities of the department for the academic year in accordance with the established procedure and deadlines, reporting at meetings of the Sciencе Council;
Conducting research work in accordance with the approved plan, ensuring the integration of completed research work with education, science and production;
Review of the educational, scientific-methodical, scientific-research, spiritual-educational and organizational-methodical work of the department's teachers in their personal work plans, study, generalize the activities of experienced teachers, and organize work aimed at popularizing their best practices;
Introduce modern pedagogical and information and communication technologies, assist young teachers who have just started teaching in acquiring pedagogical qualifications and skills, create an information base, establish the effective use of modern educational and technical means, individual teaching and independent learning methods in training sessions, make proposals for organizing training in special classrooms equipped with modern computer equipment, and carry out work on developing elements of distance learning;
To participate in expert groups to train scientific and pedagogical personnel, discuss candidate and doctoral dissertations in scientific seminars in specialties corresponding to the department's specialty or related to it, and determine whether educational documents meet the requirements of the standards;
To establish contacts with industrial enterprises and other enterprises in order to allocate graduates to work, formulate topics for graduation and qualification work, master's dissertations, attract extrabudgetary funds, as well as provide scientific and technical assistance;
To conduct events aimed at improving the qualifications of specialists in the department's specialty, and develop the system of providing educational services.
